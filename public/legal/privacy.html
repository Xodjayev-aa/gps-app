Draft Privacy Policy

Draft for preview use — operator details and legal review required before international public launch.

Controller and contact — launch blocker

The operator/controller’s legal name, postal address where required and privacy/support email have not been provided. Do not launch publicly until these are supplied and this notice is reviewed for each target market. Version: 2026-10-16.1. This draft is not a statement of GDPR or worldwide legal compliance.

Device-only data

Display name, saved destination labels/addresses, reusable errand templates, selected interface language, trip summaries (label, start, duration, distance, mode), errands (task/place), preferences and the accepted terms version/date are saved in browser storage. Live coordinates, speed and accuracy are processed to show location and estimate distance. Full GPS traces are not saved to trip history. Active trips are memory-only and can be lost on restart. Anyone with access to the browser may access local data.

Location and outside services

The app requests browser GPS only after you choose location access. You may browse without GPS and can withdraw location access in Privacy or browser settings. Tiles initially show Tashkent even before location consent. OpenFreeMap receives IP, browser request information, requested tile coordinates and style/font requests; Nominatim receives submitted address searches; OSRM receives route coordinates including requested stops. These requests require internet access and disclose information to independent providers. Do not enter sensitive addresses unnecessarily.

Optional accounts and backups

When configured, Supabase processes your email, authentication/session information, terms-version metadata and infrastructure logs. Session tokens are persisted in this browser; signing out on shared devices is important. Trip summaries, errands, saved destinations and templates are uploaded only when you explicitly choose Upload backup. A manual backup is readable by the configured service/operator subject to access controls; it is encrypted in transit, not end-to-end encrypted. Downloading replaces local trips, errands, saved destinations and templates rather than merging it.

Storage, retention and deletion

Up to 50 summaries, 10 current errands, 20 saved destinations and 20 templates are retained locally until you delete them, clear storage or replace them. An uploaded cloud snapshot remains until replaced or the cloud account is deleted. Profile offers account deletion, which removes the authentication account and cascades the active backup row. Local copies and exported files are separate: clear those yourself. Hosting/provider logs and disaster-recovery backups follow the configured providers’ retention policies, which the operator must document before launch. Account deletion does not guarantee immediate erasure from provider disaster-recovery media.

Cookies, security and offline support

No app analytics, advertising or marketing trackers are included. Browser storage supports preferences and optional authentication; a service worker caches only the app shell and legal documents, not third-party map tiles, search results or cloud requests. Standard connection security and database row-level policies help protect data but no system is risk-free. Do not share sign-in codes.

Rights, legal basis and international transfers

Use Export trips, Delete trip, Clear device data and Delete cloud account to manage data. Depending on your location you may also have access, correction, objection, restriction, portability, complaint and other rights. The operator must specify a contact, legal bases for each processing purpose, processors, hosting regions, transfer safeguards and a rights-request procedure before public launch. Acceptance of terms is not blanket consent to every use of personal data. No sale of data or public location sharing is implemented.
